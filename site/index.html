OpenCamraHub Source on GitHub

Your camera, framed for the call.

OpenCamraHub is a Mac app that turns the camera you already have into a better webcam. Zoom into the part of the frame that matters, fix the picture, and switch looks and lights with one key. Choose {{CAMERA}} as the camera in Teams, Zoom or Meet.

A 4K camera is a zoom lens in disguise

A call only ever receives 1080p. A camera that captures more than that has pixels to spare, and OpenCamraHub spends them on zoom: the crop fills the picture with real detail instead of stretching a smaller one.

The app tells you how far that reaches. The inspector says Stays sharp up to 2.0× for a 4K source, and a small soft badge appears on the picture the moment you zoom past it.

What you get

Zoom and pan into any area
Scroll or pinch over the picture to zoom around the pointer, drag to pan, double-click to reset. Or type an exact value.
Scenes instead of settings
Camera, zoom, look and overlay save into the selected scene as you change them. ⌃⌥1 to ⌃⌥9 switch scenes from anywhere, mid-call.
Pause without leaving the call
⌃⌥P blacks out the picture and hands the camera back, so its light goes out. The call never sees the camera disappear.
Elgato Key Lights built in
Found on your network automatically. Power, brightness and colour temperature from the app, and a scene can bring its own lighting.
Picture controls a capture card lacks
Exposure, black and white point, contrast, white balance, tints and saturation, for HDMI grabbers like a Cam Link that expose none.
A logo or lower third
A PNG overlay with transparency, dragged into place in the picture or snapped to one of nine positions.

OpenCamraHub only frames a live camera. It does not record, does not touch audio, and has no accounts. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Elgato or Corsair.

Keys that know what is live

A plugin for OpenDeck and the Elgato Stream Deck puts scenes, pause, zoom and your key lights on real keys. The keys follow the app: switch a scene with a hotkey or in the window, and the deck lights up the new one.

Press the live scene again to pause. Every key's label is yours to set, with placeholders for the scene, the light and the zoom.

Get it

  1. Download the disk image and drag the app into your Applications folder. macOS only activates a camera extension from there.
  2. Open it and approve the camera extension in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.
  3. Quit and reopen Teams, Zoom or Meet, and choose {{CAMERA}} as the camera.

Privacy

OpenCamraHub collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and the picture never leaves your Mac except through the call you put it in. The app checks GitHub once a day for a newer version, and talks to Elgato Key Lights on your local network if you have any. This page sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
